Clinical Consequences of the Reverse Urea Effect

🧠

Intracranial Hypertension

Since the brain retains urea while plasma urea drops, the osmotic gradient pulls water into the brain. In a rigid skull, even a small increase in brain volume raises intracranial pressure significantly.

💉

Intradialytic Hypotension

Water shifts from the intravascular space into cells (including brain cells) along the osmotic gradient. This reduces effective circulating volume even when no fluid is being ultrafiltered, causing hemodynamic instability.

⚠️

Dialysis Disequilibrium Syndrome

The clinical manifestation of severe cerebral edema from rapid urea clearance. Symptoms range from headache and nausea to seizures and coma. Most common during a patient's first dialysis session with very high BUN.
